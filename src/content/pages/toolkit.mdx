It comes in four parts, and each will get its own page when it's ready.

- **Transforming** – why building an operating model is a change project rather than an editorial one, and which roles and skills you'll need more of, and which less
- **Concepts to borrow** – what content teams can take from devops (we've made a start on [what content ops can learn from devops](https://contentious.ltd/resources/what-content-ops-can-learn-from-devops)), and what the last round of digital transformation should have taught us
- **Diagnosis** – how to work out where you are now, starting with five questions worth asking at board level
- **Design** – what to write down once you know, with a template for each of the {CELLS.length} cells

Until those pages are ready, the [framework](/framework) is the specification. If you'd like to know where your organisation stands today, the [Content Maturity assessment](https://contentmaturity.com) takes about twenty minutes and measures the system that produces your content.
