The framework goes public at 0.9 rather than 1.0 deliberately. The three layers, the seven questions and the rule and call distinction are settled and have been load-bearing in consultancy work for years.

Still being tested with real organisations: the worked templates for each question, and where the boundaries of a model should sit in organisations of very different sizes. Nothing gets called canonical until teams that don't work for us have used it.

If 1.0 differs from this, that is the ratchet working.
